THE LONG WAY AROUND

EARLY YEARS

Learning with my hands

Before any of this had a name, there was a job site, a set of tools, and a willingness to figure things out by doing them wrong first.

Lesson: craftsmanship is just repeated honesty about your own mistakes.
STARTING OUT

Iron Nation Contracting

Turned hands-on skill into a real business — renovations, repairs, and the unglamorous work that actually builds trust.

Lesson: reputation is built one finished job at a time, not one pitch.
THE DETOUR

Falling into code

Curiosity about software turned into late nights teaching myself to build. The same instincts from the job site — measure twice, ship once — carried straight over.

Lesson: the tools change. The discipline doesn't.
RIGHT NOW

Building in both worlds at once

Contracting by day, software and AI experiments by night, a podcast to hold the whole story together. Solen and AlphaQuote are the current proof of the idea.

Lesson: you don't need the entire road. You need the next step.
